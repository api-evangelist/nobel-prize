The Nobel Prize lessons are published here after the Nobel Prize announcements in October each year. Teachers can easily introduce their students to the Nobel Prize awarded achievements using a slideshow with a speaker’s manuscript. Each lesson also includes videos, a student text, assignments and links to further reading.
